TEDDY sees two cells as the same. They aren’t. Here’s how ANM catches it.

TEDDY’s embedding puts some myeloid and T cells at cosine 0.982, so a readout built on it falsely agrees 85% of the time. ANM’s decision loop vetoes the embedding for these pairs, swaps in the missing evidence (measured protein as typed evidence) with the strict rule, and checks the same pairs again. TEDDY is not retrained.

The loop, one step at a time

Click a step or use ← / → keys. Every number below is copied from the run’s stats files. Dotted words explain themselves on hover or tap.

1 · What TEDDY alone assumes

A frozen RNA → protein map. A readout on top treats cells with nearby embeddings as the same biology.

RNA→ frozen TEDDY-G→ embedding (z_512)→ predicted proteins→ soft rule→ call

What the embedding is

TEDDY’s 512-number summary of a cell: the mean of its last-layer tokens at context 1024, L2-normalized (z_rna_512.npy, 16,750 held-out cells). Not the pretraining context 2048, not a disease token, not the compact 32-number export.

The implicit promise

If two cells’ embeddings have cosine ≥ 0.98, the readout gives both the same call. TEDDY’s phase-1 Pearson ≈ 0.61 is a reference; this page does not try to beat it.
The question: is TEDDY’s embedding enough for the readout? The cheapest test of it, the must-separate check, was the strongest of four cheap probes (score 0.581, rank 1 in CHEAP_PROBES_RANKING).

What ANM adds, compared with just swapping features

If measured protein is the fix, why not glue it onto the embedding? Because distances that separate one fixed list are not a readout that owns its decisions.

Claim boundary

“Reverse loop” here is a decision loop: we change what the readout sees and asks, not TEDDY’s representation.

Claimed · the decision layer

  • Veto → complement → verify on must-separate pairs
  • TEDDY’s embedding alone is not enough for the readout on these pairs
  • Measured protein as typed complement evidence, in this explicit test only
  • A rewritten question (the strict rule) without retraining TEDDY
  • In normal use measured protein stays the answer key

Not claimed

  • Studying TEDDY’s internals: residual stream, layer Jacobians, in-silico gene perturbations (Mode A)
  • The loop as TEDDY’s representation responding to perturbations
  • Pearson above phase-1 ≈ 0.61
  • Fusion audit · clinical superiority
  • Retraining TEDDY (best.pt)
On 5,000 must-separate pairs (1,490 myeloid↔T, median cosine 0.982), TEDDY’s embedding alone is not enough for the readout: false agreement 0.85, soft separation 0.17, accuracy 0.74. ANM’s decision loop vetoes the embedding for these pairs, swaps in measured protein as typed evidence with the strict rule, and verifies false agreement → 0.40, soft separation → 0.63, accuracy → 0.99, without retraining TEDDY. Gluing features onto the embedding is not enough (global change −0.27). Not a study of TEDDY’s internals, not a Pearson claim, not clinical.
