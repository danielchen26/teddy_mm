照审计的定位去修，头多丢的部分去掉八到九成Repair where the audit points, and most of the loss goes

审计说 PD-L1 的响应丢在头的第一段网络（MLP）。这一页分开回答两个问题。定位对不对：照它去修，也照它排除的地方去修，比较两种修法各去掉多少。修好了没有：修过的头还多不多丢，原来的任务有没有弄坏。第一个问题有明确的答案，第二个还没有。The audit says the PD-L1 response is lost in the first network of the head (its MLP). This page answers two questions separately. Is the location right: repair there, repair where it rules out, and compare how much each removes. Is the head repaired: does a changed head still add a loss, and is its original task intact. The first has a clear answer. The second does not yet.

1

定位对不对：两种修法的对比Is the location right: two repairs compared

每套数据 5 个靶基因轮流留出一个：头在另外 4 个基因的敲除细胞上修，在留出基因的敲除细胞上审计；一半对照细胞不参加任何拟合，只用来审计（黑色素瘤 1,171 个敲除细胞、2,000 个对照；THP-1 5,092 个、1,193 个）。修法是在现有的头上微调，目标是实测的 PD-L1。一种只动 MLP（审计指到的那一步），解码器逐位不变；另一种只动解码器，用同样的细胞、损失和步数。Each data set's 5 target genes are held out in turn: the head is repaired on knockout cells of the other 4 and audited on those of the held-out gene; half of the control cells take part in no fit and are used only for the audit (melanoma 1,171 knockout cells and 2,000 controls; THP-1 5,092 and 1,193). A repair fine-tunes the existing head towards measured PD-L1. One moves only the MLP, the step the audit names, with the decoder bit for bit unchanged. The other moves only the decoder, with the same cells, loss and budget.

  • 定位到的那一步是要害。黑色素瘤上，原来的头在 Z 到输出之间多丢 0.39（u）。修 MLP 去掉其中 84%（74% 到 98%），修解码器去掉 5%（−4% 到 13%）。THP-1 上是 91%（85% 到 99%）对 15%（8% 到 21%）。两种修法之差的区间都在零以上。The located step is the one that matters. On melanoma the head as trained adds 0.39 to u between Z and its output. Repairing the MLP removes 84% of it (74% to 98%); repairing the decoder removes 5% (−4% to 13%). On THP-1: 91% (85% to 99%) against 15% (8% to 21%). The interval of the difference between the two repairs lies above zero on both.
  • 逐个基因。10 个留出基因里 9 个过了准入（THP-1 的 IRF1 响应太小），9 个都读作“定位得到确认”。Gene by gene. Of the 10 held-out genes 9 pass admission (the response of IRF1 on THP-1 is too small), and all 9 read “located step confirmed”.
  • 丢失出现的地方，和能把它去掉的地方，不是同一处。黑色素瘤上原来的头在两步都多丢：Z 到中间层 0.16，中间层到输出 0.23。只修 MLP、解码器不动，第二步就只多丢 0.02；只修解码器、MLP 不动，第二步照样多丢 0.21。Where the loss shows and where it can be removed are not the same place. On melanoma the head as trained adds a loss at both of its steps: 0.16 from Z to the latent and 0.23 from the latent to the output. With only the MLP repaired and the decoder untouched, the second step adds 0.02. With only the decoder repaired and the MLP untouched, it still adds 0.21.
  • 只看终点的平均值会选错。只修解码器后，头自己的 PD-L1 输出在没见过的敲除上平均下降了实测的 1.15 到 1.29 倍（黑色素瘤；原来只有 0.41 到 0.45），看上去修好了。审计仍然否定它，因为它读的中间层没有变。The average at the endpoint would pick the wrong repair. With only the decoder repaired, the head's own PD-L1 output falls for unseen knockouts by 1.15 to 1.29 times the measured fall (melanoma; 0.41 to 0.45 as trained), which looks repaired. The audit still rejects it, because the latent it reads from has not changed.
  • 没训练的读出。THP-1 上的 PD-L2 没有对应的头输出，从没进过损失；为 PD-L1 做的 MLP 修复把它多丢的部分也去掉了（104%，66% 到 117%），解码器修复去掉 25%。A readout that was not trained. PD-L2 on THP-1 has no output in the head and was in no loss; the MLP repair made for PD-L1 removes its added loss as well (104%, 66% to 117%), and the decoder repair removes 25%.

修好了没有：修过的头还多不多丢Is it repaired: does a changed head still add a loss

这是另一个问题，要的证据也不同。上一节只要“这种修法比那种去掉得多”；这一节要“丢失不在了”。读法分三档，从强到弱：头的输出被判“支持”，这是论文里的闭环；确认没有多丢，即头多丢的部分连同区间整个在 0.10 以下；丢失不再定得清，即区间跨过 0.10。This is a different question and it needs different evidence. The section above needs only “this repair removes more than that one”. This one needs “the loss is gone”. Three grades, strongest first: the head's output is supported, which is the closing of a loop in the paper's sense; no added loss shown, where what the head adds lies wholly below 0.10, interval included; the loss is no longer resolved, where the interval crosses 0.10.

代价：头原来的任务The cost: the head's original task

头原来的任务是在骨髓细胞上从 RNA 预测 134 个蛋白。第 1 轮的修复把这项得分从 0.602 拉到 0.502–0.567，超过事先定的 0.02。第 2 轮在损失里加一项，把头在 3,000 个骨髓训练细胞上的输出按在原处；这一项的权重只在骨髓验证细胞上选。头多丢的部分照样去掉，得分是 0.594–0.604。The head's original task is predicting 134 proteins from RNA on bone-marrow cells. The repair of round 1 pulled that score from 0.602 to 0.502–0.567, beyond the 0.02 set in advance. Round 2 adds one term to the loss that holds the head's outputs on 3,000 bone-marrow training cells where they are; its weight is chosen on bone-marrow validation cells only. The added loss still goes, and the score is 0.594–0.604.

第 3 轮：一个头，两种细胞Round 3: one head for both cell types

第 2 轮之后审计还指出两处，都在头里、都指向同一步：黑色素瘤上的 HLA-E，以及换一种细胞就失效。所以这一轮还是修 MLP，但把缺的补进拟合：两种细胞放在一起修成一个头，凡是实测了、头又有对应输出的蛋白都训（黑色素瘤上 PD-L1、MHC-I、HLA-E，THP-1 上 PD-L1）。六个靶基因轮流留出，在两套数据里同时留出。After round 2 the audit still located two things, both in the head and both at the same step: HLA-E on melanoma, and the failure on the other cell type. So this round repairs the MLP again and adds what the fit lacked: both cell types in one fit, giving one head, and every measured protein the head has an output for is trained (PD-L1, MHC-I and HLA-E on melanoma; PD-L1 on THP-1). The six target genes are held out in turn, in both data sets at once.

  • 按这一轮事先写的规则，没有通过。黑色素瘤：PD-L1 在头的输出上是 0.17，Z 处 0.06，头多丢 0.109，过了 0.10 的界限，记为退步（第 2 轮是 0.12）。换两次对照细胞的划分重做，也都没过（0.111 和 0.140）。THP-1：三次划分都通过（0.17，Z 处 0.12）。六个头在骨髓上是 0.596–0.598，在界限内。所以保留第 2 轮的修法，每种细胞一个头；“一个头管两种细胞”没有成立。By the rule written for this round, it did not pass. On melanoma PD-L1 is 0.17 at the head output against 0.06 at Z: the head adds 0.109, over the margin of 0.10, which counts as regressed (round 2: 0.12). Repeated on two fresh splits of the control cells it does not pass either (0.111 and 0.140). THP-1 passes on all three splits (0.17, with 0.12 at Z). The six heads score 0.596–0.598 on bone marrow, within the guard. So the repair of round 2 stands, with one head per cell type; one head for both cell types is not established.
  • 和第 2 轮的头直接比。两个头在同一批细胞上审计，可以配对比较。黑色素瘤上第 3 轮的头比第 2 轮多丢 0.044（区间 [0.002, 0.090]），区间刚好不含零。THP-1 上没有定得清的差别（−0.01，[−0.053, 0.022]）。三次划分上黑色素瘤多丢的部分是 0.109、0.111、0.140。Compared directly with the head of round 2. The two heads are audited on the same cells, so the comparison is paired. On melanoma the head of round 3 adds 0.044 more than that of round 2 (interval [0.002, 0.090]), an interval that just excludes zero. On THP-1 no difference is resolved (−0.01, [−0.053, 0.022]). On the three splits the added loss on melanoma is 0.109, 0.111, 0.140.
  • HLA-E 没有修好，而且本来就不该当目标。黑色素瘤上的 HLA-E 仍被指出在头里丢（输出 0.96，第 2 轮 1.04）。头自己的 HLA-E 输出在这些敲除上是升的（修复后 +0.77 到 +0.96 个对照标准差），实测蛋白略降（−0.23 到 −0.06）；在拟合用过的基因上也一样是升的（+0.80 到 +0.88）。也就是说，这次拟合在 400 步之内连训练过的基因上都没有学到 HLA-E 的响应，所以它说明的是这次拟合修不好，不是 MLP 一定带不动；原因没有查。按后来写进协议的准入检查，HLA-E 和 MHC-I 在这批细胞上的响应相对自己的区间太小（1.9 和 2.9，要求 3），跟不住。按停止规则，不再换别的拟合去试。HLA-E is not repaired, and should not have been a target. HLA-E on melanoma is still located in the head (output 0.96; round 2: 1.04). The head's own HLA-E output rises on these knockouts (+0.77 to +0.96 control standard deviations after the repair) while the measured protein falls slightly (−0.23 to −0.06); it rises just as much on the genes the fit was trained on (+0.80 to +0.88). So within its 400 steps this fit did not learn the HLA-E response even where it was trained on it: the round shows that this fit does not repair HLA-E, not that the MLP cannot; the reason was not examined. By the admission check written into the protocol afterwards, the response of HLA-E and of MHC-I on these cells is small against its own interval (1.9 and 2.9, where 3 is wanted): they could not be followed. By the stopping rule no further variant of the fit is tried.

现在该用哪个头Which head to use now

第 2 轮的修法：每种细胞一个头，只训 PD-L1。它通过了当时的规则，直接比较也不比第 3 轮差。scripts/07_repair_head.py 用 teddy_mm 自己的模型代码做同样的修复；它做出的头和这里审计过的头对过，输出相差不到 0.001（修复本身带来的变化是 0.06 和 0.20）。用它在全部基因上各修一个头，在完整的骨髓测试集（16,750 个细胞）上，teddy_mm 自己的评估是 0.604（黑色素瘤的头）和 0.596（THP-1 的头），原来 0.603。这两个头本身没有审计过，因为它们就是在这些敲除细胞上修的；证据是留出基因的那些头。一个头只对修它时用的那种细胞有依据。第 3 轮的联合头（0.598）不建议用。这些都是微调副本，离“修好”还差上面第 2 节说的那些。The repair of round 2: one head per cell type, trained on PD-L1 only. It passed the rule of its time and is no worse than round 3 in the direct comparison. scripts/07_repair_head.py makes the same repair with teddy_mm's own model code; its heads were compared with the ones audited here and their outputs differ by less than 0.001 (the repair itself moves them by 0.06 and 0.20). Fitted with it on all genes, one head per cell type, they score 0.604 (the melanoma head) and 0.596 (the THP-1 head) in teddy_mm's own evaluation on the full bone-marrow test split (16,750 cells), against 0.603 as trained. These two heads are not audited themselves, because they were repaired on these knockout cells; the evidence is the heads with a gene held out. A head is supported only for the cell type it was repaired on. The joint head of round 3 (0.598) is not recommended. All of these are fine-tuned copies, and what section 2 lists still stands between them and “repaired”.

接下来怎么做才不白做：协议和没用过的数据What to do next without wasting it: the protocol and unused data

这三轮都是用一个点值去比 0.10 来判定的，每一轮都在看过上一轮之后设计，同一批审计细胞用了三次。闭环协议把这些补上：每一步花钱之前先过一道便宜的关。All three rounds were decided by comparing a point value with 0.10, each was designed after the previous one was read, and the same audit cells served three times. The loop protocol closes these gaps: a cheap gate stands before every step that costs something.

这些结果没有说明什么What these results do not show

  • 头修好了。没有一个修过的头得到“支持”；黑色素瘤上连“没有多丢”都没有确认。That the head is repaired. No changed head is supported, and on melanoma not even the absence of an added loss is shown.
  • 修复能带到别的细胞类型。第 1 轮里，在一种细胞上修的头到另一种细胞上仍然丢（多丢 0.39 和 0.26）。把两种细胞放进同一个拟合的第 3 轮，在黑色素瘤上比分开修略差。没有审计过第三种细胞。That a repair carries to another cell type. In round 1 a head repaired on one cell type still loses the response on the other (it adds 0.39 and 0.26). Round 3, with both cell types in one fit, is slightly worse on melanoma than the separate repair. No third cell type has been audited.
  • 这是封存的第 4 步。那条规则要三个训练种子、一组只差这处改动的对照重训，并要求头的中间层得到“支持”。这里是在现有的头上微调，一个训练种子，比的是原来的头。That this is the sealed step 4. That rule asks for three training seeds, a control retrain that differs only by the change, and “support” at the head latent. Here the existing head is fine-tuned, with one training seed, and compared with the head as trained.
  • 证据等级：探索性。每套数据 5 个基因作重抽样单位，区间是近似的。按区间的读法是事后做的，每一轮当时按自己的规则得到的结论原样保留在各自的记录里。Evidence grade: exploratory. Five genes per data set are the resampling units, so intervals are approximate. The reading by intervals was made afterwards; what each round concluded under its own rule stays as recorded in its folder.
