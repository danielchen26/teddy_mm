照审计的定位去修，丢失就没了Repair where the audit points, and the loss goes

审计说 PD-L1 的响应丢在头的第一段网络（MLP）。这一页是对这句话的检验：照它去修，再审；按它的说法不该有用的修法，也做一遍；然后看修复有没有弄坏头原来的任务。The audit says the PD-L1 response is lost in the first network of the head (its MLP). This page is the test of that statement: repair there and audit again; make the repair that should not help as well; then check whether the repair damaged the head's original task.

第 1、2 轮：修审计指到的那一步Rounds 1 and 2: repair the step the audit names

每套数据 5 个靶基因轮流留出一个：头在另外 4 个基因的敲除细胞上修，在留出基因的敲除细胞上审计；一半对照细胞不参加任何拟合，只用来审计（黑色素瘤 1,171 个敲除细胞、2,000 个对照；THP-1 5,092 个、1,193 个）。修法是在现有的头上微调：只动 MLP 的三层，解码器逐位不变；目标是实测的 PD-L1。Each data set's 5 target genes are held out in turn: the head is repaired on knockout cells of the other 4 and audited on those of the held-out gene; half of the control cells take part in no fit and are used only for the audit (melanoma 1,171 knockout cells and 2,000 controls; THP-1 5,092 and 1,193). The repair fine-tunes the existing head: only the three MLP layers move, the decoder stays bit for bit; the target is measured PD-L1.

  • 按规则，两种细胞上都在定位到的那一步闭环。输出上的 u：黑色素瘤 0.45 → 0.12，只修解码器是 0.43；THP-1 0.54 → 0.16，只修解码器是 0.47。By the rule, the loop closes at the located step on both cell types. u at the output: melanoma 0.45 → 0.12, against 0.43 with only the decoder repaired; THP-1 0.54 → 0.16, against 0.47.
  • 逐个基因。原来的头，10 个留出基因都被指出在头里丢；只修解码器后还是 10 个；修 MLP 后 0 个（5 个头不再丢，5 个没定清）。Gene by gene. As trained, the tool names a head step for all 10 held-out genes; with only the decoder repaired, still 10; with the MLP repaired, 0 (5 read “no loss in the head” and 5 “not resolved”).
  • 只看终点的平均值会选错。只修解码器后，头自己的 PD-L1 输出在没见过的敲除上平均下降了实测的 1.15 到 1.29 倍（黑色素瘤；原来只有 0.41 到 0.45），看上去修好了。审计仍然否定它，因为它读的中间层没有变。The average at the endpoint would pick the wrong repair. With only the decoder repaired, the head's own PD-L1 output falls for unseen knockouts by 1.15 to 1.29 times the measured fall (melanoma; 0.41 to 0.45 as trained), which looks repaired. The audit still rejects it, because the latent it reads from has not changed.

代价：头原来的任务The cost: the head's original task

头原来的任务是在骨髓细胞上从 RNA 预测 134 个蛋白。第 1 轮的修复把这项得分从 0.602 拉到 0.502–0.567，超过事先定的 0.02。第 2 轮在损失里加一项，把头在 3,000 个骨髓训练细胞上的输出按在原处；这一项的权重只在骨髓验证细胞上选。丢失照样去掉，得分是 0.594–0.604。The head's original task is predicting 134 proteins from RNA on bone-marrow cells. The repair of round 1 pulled that score from 0.602 to 0.502–0.567, beyond the 0.02 set in advance. Round 2 adds one term to the loss that holds the head's outputs on 3,000 bone-marrow training cells where they are; its weight is chosen on bone-marrow validation cells only. The loss still goes, and the score is 0.594–0.604.

这些结果没有说明什么What these results do not show

  • 修好的头没有被判“支持”。它只是不比 Z 多丢：这批细胞上 Z 自己就没定清（0.06 和 0.12）。The repaired head is not given “support”. It only adds no loss beyond Z: on these cells Z itself is unresolved (0.06 and 0.12).
  • 修复不会自己带到别的细胞类型。第 1 轮里，在一种细胞上修的头到另一种细胞上仍被指出在头里丢（0.40 和 0.43）。没有审计过第三种细胞。A repair does not carry to another cell type by itself. In round 1 a head repaired on one cell type is still located in the head on the other (0.40 and 0.43). No third cell type has been audited.
  • 这还不是封存的第 4 步。那条规则要三个训练种子、一组只差这处改动的对照重训，并要求头的中间层得到“支持”。这里是在现有的头上微调，比的是原来的头。This is not yet the sealed step 4. That rule asks for three training seeds, a control retrain that differs only by the change, and “support” at the head latent. Here the existing head is fine-tuned and compared with the head as trained.
  • 证据等级：探索性。每套数据 5 个基因作重抽样单位，区间只能看方向。每一轮都是看过上一轮的结果之后才设计的；设计和规则在各自运行之前提交。Evidence grade: exploratory. Five genes per data set are the resampling units, so intervals show direction only. Each round was designed after the previous one was read; its design and rule were committed before it ran.
