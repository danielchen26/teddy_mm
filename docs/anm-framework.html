01

The idea

1.1 · The idea

A score says it failed, not where

Pipelines are usually judged by one final score. A wrong answer tells you something failed, not where: which input caused it, whether the information was lost in the carried state or ignored at the output, and whether the system is degraded or broken.

只看最终分数，只知道错了，不知道错在哪：是哪个输入，信息丢在中间状态还是读出没用上，是变差了还是已经坏了。

Sources
  • Paper · sections/introduction.tex 3–10, 20–25

1.2 · The idea

Push, follow, read, compare

ANM is a measuring protocol. Push one source by a known amount, follow the change through a declared candidate retained state, read a declared output, and compare it with a boundary declared in advance: ‘still works’ and the stricter ‘exactly right’.

ANM 是一套测量协议：给源一个已知大小的推动，跟着它走过事先声明的候选保留状态，看声明的读出，再和事先声明的边界（还能用 / 完全正确）比较。

Sources
  • Paper · sections/introduction.tex 20–25
  • Paper · sections/protocol.tex 69–79
  • Paper · appendix/theory.tex 1036–1062 (nested readouts)

1.3 · The idea

Forward: where lost. Inverse: is it enough?

Forward: if the state moves but the output stays flat, the information arrived and the readout does not use it, or cancels it. Inverse: if two inputs reach the same state but give different outputs, the state is missing something the output depends on.

正向：状态动了、输出不动，说明信息到了，但读出没用上（或被抵消）。逆向：两个输入到了同一个状态却给出不同输出，说明状态漏了输出所依赖的东西。

Sources
  • Paper · sections/introduction.tex 24–31
  • Paper · sections/protocol.tex 156–181
  • Paper · appendix/theory.tex 695–708 (a state change with no endpoint change can mean cancellation or robustness; an association is not mediation)
02

The maths

2.1 · The maths

The response along the real run

Along the actual run, a small push at step t enters through Bt, is carried forward by each later step’s A, and is read at the end. The output change is the sum of every push, each carried to the end. No equilibrium needed.

\[\delta s_t = A_t\,\delta s_{t-1} + B_t\,\delta u_t\]
\[A_t=\mathrm D_sF_t,\ B_t=\mathrm D_uF_t\]
\[\delta O_T = \mathrm D_sO_T(s_T)\sum_{t=1}^{T}\Phi(T,t)\,B_t\,\delta u_t\]
\[\Phi(T,t)=A_T\cdots A_{t+1},\ \Phi(T,T)=I\]

沿着系统真实的运行轨迹，第 t 步的小推动经 B_t 注入，被之后每一步的 A 传下去，最后被读出。输出变化是所有推动各自传到终点后的和，不需要平衡态。

Sources
  • Paper · sections/protocol.tex 69–93 (finite-graph linear response; finite-window endpoint response)
  • Paper · sections/protocol.tex 123–154 (a stationary susceptibility is only the special case that needs closure and stability)
  • Paper · appendix/theory.tex 614–622 (full tangent recursion, with the initial-history term)

2.2 · The maths

Two checks: linearity and nested readouts

Linearity is checked, not assumed: the slope measured with a push ε must match the slope with ε/2. The readouts are nested: ‘exactly right’ can only happen inside ‘still works’, so losing the first while keeping the second separates degraded from broken.

\[\frac{O(u_0+\varepsilon)-O(u_0-\varepsilon)}{2\varepsilon}\approx\frac{O(u_0+\varepsilon/2)-O(u_0-\varepsilon/2)}{\varepsilon}\]
\[0\le Q_f\le P_f\le 1\]
\[w=P_f,\ r=Q_f/P_f\]

线性是检验出来的，不是假设的：用 ε 和 ε/2 测得的斜率必须一致。读出是嵌套的：“完全正确”只能发生在“还能用”之内，所以前者丢了而后者还在，就能区分“变差”和“坏了”。

Sources
  • Paper · sections/protocol.tex 101–103 (step refinement)
  • Paper · appendix/empirical.tex 296–299, 346–351 (ε = 0.1 on baseline 0.5, ε/2 = 0.05; slopes must agree within 25%)
  • Paper · appendix/theory.tex 1036–1062 (nested observables)

2.3 · The maths

A step is any real processing order

Step t can be clock time, an iteration, an evidence arrival or a network layer, if it is the real processing order. Memory, feedback and delay live in the state and in At. A made-up order biases results; our old panel order did.

\[s_t=F_t(s_{t-1},u_t)\]
\[\text{delay }\tau:\ s_t\ni\xi^t(\theta)=c(t+\theta),\ -\tau\le\theta\le 0\]

步 t 可以是物理时间、迭代、证据到达或网络层，只要是真实的处理顺序；记忆、反馈、延迟都放进状态和 A_t。编造的顺序会带来偏差，旧桥接的面板顺序就是这样。

Physics

3.1 · Physicscalibration

Physics I: a delayed ring, measured vs theory

64 sites on a ring, each coupled to four neighbours with delay τ, are driven by a sine. The retained state must include the history. Over 13 delays, measured amplitude and phase stayed within 2.51×10⁻³ (relative) and 0.74° of theory: a calibration of the measurement.

\[\dot c_i(t)=-a\,c_i(t)+\frac{K}{4}\sum_{j\in N_i}c_j(t-\tau)+F\cos(\Omega t)+\sigma\xi_i(t)\]
\[\hat Z=\frac{2}{T_{\rm win}}\int_{\rm window} m(t)\,e^{-i\Omega t}\,dt\]
\[m(t)=\tfrac{1}{64}\textstyle\sum_i c_i(t)\]

64 个位点的环，每个与 4 个邻居以延迟 τ 耦合，受正弦驱动，保留状态必须包含历史。13 个延迟上，测得的幅度和相位与解析解相差不超过 2.51×10⁻³（相对）和 0.74°。这是对测量本身的校准。

Sources
  • Paper · sections/physics.tex 17–37, table rows 53–54
  • Paper · appendix/empirical.tex 896–925 (a = 1, K = 0.40, F = 0.25, Ω = 0.35, σ = 0.015, τ ∈ [0.43, 0.46])
  • Paper · appendix/empirical.tex 1075–1087 (maxima of 100-seed means over 13 delays, including estimator leakage and discretization)

3.2 · Physics

Physics II: two flock readouts fail apart

256 particles align with neighbours and a field while angular noise η rises. Alignment within 30° of the field is first below one half at η = 0.35, polarization only at 0.60. Alignment fell first at all three tested tolerances; the readouts are not nested.

\[\theta_i(t+1)=\operatorname{Arg}\Big(\sum_{j\in N_i}e^{i\theta_j(t)}+h\,e^{i\theta_{\hat e}}\Big)+\eta\,\xi_i(t)\]
\[P_f=\Big\langle\Big|\tfrac{1}{256}\sum_i e^{i\theta_i}\Big|\Big\rangle\]
\[Q_f=\big\langle\text{share with }|\theta_i-\theta_{\hat e}|\le 30^\circ\big\rangle\]

256 个粒子与邻居和外场对齐，角噪声 η 逐渐升高。30° 以内的对齐比例在 η=0.35 首次低于一半，极化到 0.60 才低于一半。三种测试容差下都是对齐先跌；两个读出不是嵌套的。

Sources
  • Paper · sections/physics.tex 79–92, table rows 62–63
  • Paper · appendix/empirical.tex 1280–1300 (N = 256, box L = 8, field h = 0.6 at 0°)
  • Paper · appendix/empirical.tex 1361–1384 (tolerances 20°/30°/45° give 0.25/0.35/0.50 against 0.60 for polarization; the order can reverse near 45.5°)

4.1 · AI workflows

AI step 1: write the question down

First declare the observer: what is read, which answers are allowed, and the boundary. ‘Still works’ = an allowed answer cleared the bar; ‘exactly right’ = it matches the key. The key grades only after the readout and never enters the evidence.

\[\text{call}=\begin{cases}\arg\max_a S_a & \max_a S_a\ge\theta\\ \text{no call} & \text{otherwise}\end{cases}\]
\[P_f=\mathbb 1[\text{call made}]\]
\[Q_f=\mathbb 1[\text{call}=a^{\ast}]\]
\[r_f=Q_f/P_f\]

先写下观察者：读什么、允许哪些答案、边界在哪里。“还能用”是有答案过了门槛，“完全正确”是答案与答案标准一致。答案标准只在读出之后打分，从不进入证据。

Sources
  • ANM · active_neural_matter/field/finite_field_runner.py 925–975 (the best action is the call if it clears the threshold, otherwise no call), 978–998 (verify: P_f, Q_f, r_f)
  • teddy_mm · bridge_anm/schemas/cite_lineage_finite_field_v0.json 51–74 (no-leakage rule), 93–102 (observables, verifier)
  • Paper · sections/worked_example.tex 13–22

4.2 · AI workflows

AI step 2: build the typed event graph

Each record becomes a typed event (time, target answer, type, sign, value, provenance), admitted, repaired or rejected by a schema. Event nodes link to answer nodes; each step retains and spreads values, open-loop. States are specified from outside; no live multi-agent claim.

\[s'=\rho\,s_t+\beta\,b_t\]
\[s_{t+1}=(I+\kappa W^{\top})\,s'\]
\[b_t[e]=\begin{cases}+v_e&\text{support}\\-v_e&\text{contradiction}\\+0.25\,v_e&\text{uncertainty reduction}\end{cases}\]
\[W_{e\to a}=1,\ W_{a\to e}=0.15\]

每条记录变成一个类型化事件（时间、目标答案、类型、符号、数值、来源），由模式接收、修复或拒绝。事件节点连到答案节点；每一步保留一部分值并沿边扩散，开环。状态由外部指定，不声称是在线多智能体形成的状态。

  • Paper · sections/ai_evidence.tex 4–12; sections/introduction.tex 39–46

4.3 · AI workflows

AI step 3: declare a candidate state, then test it

The retained state is a candidate declared before the run; its choice does not establish sufficiency. It is enough only if equal states always give equal readouts. With delay, two histories with the same present value but different pasts evolve differently, so the present value is not enough. A same-state violation shows what the state must separate.

\[\mathsf z_T(u_1)=\mathsf z_T(u_2)\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathsf o_T(u_1)=\mathsf o_T(u_2)\]
\[\ker\mathcal K_{z,T}\subseteq\ker\mathcal K_{O,T}\iff\mathcal K_{O,T}=\mathcal H\,\mathcal K_{z,T}\]
\[\dot x(t)=-a\,x(t)-k\,x(t-\tau)+u(t)\]

保留状态是运行前声明的候选坐标，声明本身不证明充分。候选状态只有在“同一状态必给同一读出”时才够用。有延迟时，当前值相同但过去不同的两段历史会走向不同结果，所以只看当前值不够。一旦出现“同状态、不同读出”，就知道状态必须区分什么。

Sources
  • Paper · sections/protocol.tex 3–11, 156–181
  • Paper · appendix/theory.tex 596–693 (retained-state criterion; delayed-history counterexample 655–670), 729–745 (inverse use)

4.4 · AI workflows

AI step 4: measure the response

Push one event’s value up and down by ε, then by ε/2, and measure how far the state moved and how far the readout moved, separately. In the graph study, fresh model readouts changed in 9 of 12 directions; only 2 passed the step-halving check. In these open-loop workflows (retention, multi-step propagation, histories, no feedback) ANM is a response-theory verifier, not a better decision-maker.

\[\Delta z_d(T)=z_{d,+}(T)-z_{d,-}(T)\]
\[\hat g_d(\varepsilon)=\frac{s_{d,+}-s_{d,-}}{2\varepsilon}\]
\[\frac{|\hat g_d(\varepsilon)-\hat g_d(\varepsilon/2)|}{\max\{|\hat g_d(\varepsilon)|,|\hat g_d(\varepsilon/2)|\}}\le 0.25\]

把一个事件的值上下各推 ε，再推 ε/2，分别测状态移动了多少、读出移动了多少。在图研究中，新鲜模型读出在 12 个方向里有 9 个发生变化，只有 2 个通过步长减半检验。在这些开环工作流（有保留、多步传播、历史，无反馈）中，ANM 是响应理论的验证器，而不是更好的决策者。

Sources
  • Paper · sections/ai_evidence.tex 62–113 (state change 12/12 by construction, readout change 9/12, slope agreement 2/12)
  • Paper · appendix/empirical.tex 295–360 (ε = 0.1 on baseline 0.5, ε/2 = 0.05; 25% tolerance)
  • Paper · appendix/theory.tex 695–708

4.5 · AI workflowsexploratory

AI case: a missing response, restored

Swapping two requests’ warehouses should move the answer 14→9; the model stayed 9→9. A request–return join, fixed in advance, restored 14→9; three controls stayed 9→9. Exploratory, one known case; calibration missed (21/24 vs 22/24); ordinary diagnosis picks the same join.

\[z_P=(\text{ALB-C},\ 10\text{ units},\ \text{rev }7):\ \text{unchanged by the swap}\]
\[z_H=(z_P,\ \text{requests A–C}):\ \text{changes}\]
\[\text{required }\Delta=9-14=-5\]

交换两个请求的仓库，答案应从 14 变成 9，模型却一直是 9→9。按事先固定的规则把每个请求与它的返回结果连接起来后，恢复为 14→9；三个对照仍是 9→9。这是探索性结果：一个已知案例，校准未达标（21/24 对 22/24），普通诊断也会选同一个连接。

5.1 · TEDDYofficial rerun

TEDDY embeds, our head predicts, ANM decides

Each CITE-seq cell’s RNA goes through frozen TEDDY-G 70M, an RNA model, giving a 512-number embedding. Our own small head, trained on training sites’ measured proteins, predicts 134 surface proteins (held-out site4 mean Pearson 0.603). ANM reads 9 of them (v2; the registered v3 tests read 12, three each for B, T, NK and myeloid, chosen on validation, and divide each prediction by the training 95th percentile of the head’s own predictions, so no measured protein enters the evidence). Measured protein only grades. Numbers come from the official TEDDY-G preprocessing (counts / total × 10⁴, divided by TEDDY’s gene medians, top 2,048 rank tokens, mean over gene tokens); the earlier non-official preprocessing gave the same phase-1 Pearson.

BCD19CD72CD22TCD3CD2CD5MyeloidCD16CD11cCD36
\[z=\operatorname{mean}_{\text{real tokens}}\,h_{12}(x_{\rm RNA})\in\mathbb R^{512}\]
\[(\mu,\theta)=\mathrm{Dec}_{\rm NB}\big(\mathrm{MLP}(z),\ \mathrm{sf}\big)\]
\[\hat y=\mu\in\mathbb R^{134}\]
\[\mathrm{sf}=0.92663\]

每个 CITE-seq 细胞的 RNA 经过冻结的 TEDDY-G 70M（一个 RNA 模型）得到 512 维嵌入。我们自己训练的小 head 用训练 site 的实测蛋白学习，预测 134 个表面蛋白（留出 site4 平均 Pearson 0.603）。ANM 只读其中 9 个（v2；v3 为 12 个，证据中不含实测蛋白），实测蛋白只用来打分。TEDDY 本身不预测蛋白。数字来自 TEDDY-G 官方预处理（计数/总数×10⁴、除以 TEDDY 基因中位数、取前 2,048 个基因排序 token、基因 token 平均）；此前的非官方预处理给出相同的 phase-1 Pearson。

5.3 · TEDDYconfirmed in v3 E1

Finding: ANM’s calls equal the fixed rule

All nine markers enter at once into a linear graph with uncoupled lineages, so each ANM score is a fixed multiple of the rule’s. On all 16,750 held-out cells and three questions, calls are identical: a check that the engine computes the rule, not better accuracy.

v3 · registeredThe same holds on the 11,294 unseen-donor test cells and the three v3 questions: 0 mismatches (E1.1a), with G(3) = 0.370041 the field’s gain for a class with 3 events. None of ANM’s readouts (top action score, closure readout, leave-one-out) beat TEDDY’s margin, the mean rule or a trained classifier, and a classifier trained on the old question’s labels answered a new question better than the zero-label rule (−0.078 [−0.134, −0.028], loss). On 40,000 external PBMC cells (E6; RNA possibly seen by TEDDY in pretraining) the engine again made the declared rule’s call in every donor. E1 → · E6 →

\[S^{\rm ANM}_l=G\,c\,S^{\rm rule}_l\]
\[S^{\rm rule}_l=\frac{1}{c}\sum_{p\in l}\tilde w_p\,v_p\]
\[\theta^{\rm ANM}=G\,c\,\theta^{\rm rule}\ \Rightarrow\ \text{same call}\]

九个标记同时进入一个线性、谱系之间互不耦合的图，所以 ANM 的分数正好是规则分数的固定倍数。在 16,750 个留出细胞、三个问题上，判定完全相同：这只是核对引擎算的就是规则，不是更高的准确率。v3 在 11,294 个未见供体细胞上再次确认（0 不一致），且 ANM 的读出均未胜过 TEDDY 的分数差或训练分类器；E6 在外部 PBMC 数据的每个供体上同样 0 不一致。

Finding: NK and T nearly merge

v2 prototype of a sufficiency test, with TEDDY’s embedding as the declared candidate state: for 453 NK–T pairs that TEDDY’s embedding nearly merges, our head’s predicted gaps are a quarter to a third of the measured ones (CD335 0.88), right in direction. Mode A probes on the frozen embedding: a linear probe keeps about as much as our head, but an MLP probe keeps about twice as much for CD56, CD94 and CD3, and gene-token states keep more than the gene-mean. v2 read this as: part of the loss is in our head’s readout and in mean pooling; part may not be in the detected RNA.

Superseded by v3 E3The registered test rejected “the NK–T loss is in the readout or pooling and is repairable on frozen TEDDY”: a layer-mean MLP (R1) and attention pooling over layer-12 gene tokens (R2) both told NK from T less accurately than our head (−0.0409 and −0.1925 at coverage 0.9, losses). E5-M then tested the gene-mean against a token observer: inconclusive, for that one observer. E3 →

\[\|\widehat{\Delta O}\|\le L_h\,\varepsilon_z+\varepsilon_O\]

near-matched states: a violation rejects the readout class, here our head, not the state alone

Nonlinear at the input. TEDDY reads genes as rank tokens, and thinning reorders them, so even small pushes change the input in jumps. TEDDY’s layers are a real processing order, so a push can be followed layer by layer (Mode A). E5 pushed each panel or NK/T gene’s input token along its own embedding on 478 unseen-donor cells (7,862 cases), carried the exact first-order change through each of the 12 post-norm layers with Jacobian-vector products, and projected it on the training NK-minus-T direction. The registered question: in which layer does the NK–T component shrink most, and is it the layer where a linear NK–T probe’s gap ratio drops most?

The registered gate failed in float32; float64 passes. Before any layer is read, finite differences must agree with the JVP in at least 95% of cases. In float32 at ε = 1e-3 they agreed in 0.854; the float64 version of the same check passes in all 79 cases, so the registered verdict attributes the failure to float32 rounding. No amendment adopting the float64 path was committed before site4, so the verdict stands: no layer is localised.

Descriptive only: for a push along the training NK − T input direction, the largest single-layer drop of its NK–T component is at layer 2 (median step −0.217 [−0.229, −0.197]); look-alike cells do not damp it more than random NK/T cells, and the head does not pass less of it than the layer-12 state.

\[\delta h_l=J_l\,\delta h_{l-1},\quad J_l=\partial f_l/\partial h\,\big|_{h_{l-1}}\]
\[\text{gate: }\frac{\|\mathrm{FD}_\varepsilon-\mathrm{JVP}\|}{\|\mathrm{JVP}\|}\le 0.05,\ \cos\ge 0.99\ \text{in}\ge 95\%\]
\[l^\ast=\arg\min_l\ \mathrm{median}\big(\log|r_l|-\log|r_{l-1}|\big)\]

What it found. For R2, a median 0.085 of its first-order response on look-alike pair cells flows through what gene-mean pooling discards, against 0.127 on random cells: smaller, not larger, on look-alikes. The random observer shows no such gap (0.067 vs 0.068). The registered verdict is inconclusive: neither “pooling loses the look-alike difference” nor “the gene-mean state is sufficient” is established, and the measurement holds for this one observer only. R2’s attention is nearly uniform (a median 1,141.957 effective of 1,368 tokens, E3), and an observer that attends almost uniformly departs little from the gene-mean by construction.

How it decided. ANM’s clamp estimator H = Λ·pinv(Γ) is computed from clamps of the candidate alone, never fitted on the tested responses. The per-cell residual is the share of an observer’s response that flows through what gene-mean pooling discards. H5M needed the pair-minus-random difference for R2 to reach 0.05 under the common win rule and to exceed the random-observer null. The readout-visible quotient is a diagnostic only, never the state: R2’s response has rank 4 in 128 cells and 3 in 62, with a median participation ratio of 1.221 on pair and 1.388 on random cells, so it is effectively one- or two-dimensional.

ANM’s own factorization check. analyze_factorization at rtol 1e-4 rejects the gene-mean as an exact first-order retained state for R2 in 195 of 195 cells; any relative residual above 1e-4 rejects, so it says nothing about size. Its source-kernel test is not informative in all 195, as the paper predicts: with at most 21 push columns against 512 coordinates, K_z has full column rank in every cell, so first-order kernel inclusion holds automatically. The informative quantity is the clamp residual of an H fixed without the tested responses.

It tests a declared candidate against declared observers. It does not let ANM “find” TEDDY’s minimal degrees of freedom.

5.9 · TEDDY

What we do not claim

Not claimed (the registered list is the v3 claims ledger):

  • that ANM makes calls more accurate: in v3 its engine equals a declared rule on every test cell, and none of its readouts beat TEDDY’s margin or a trained classifier
  • that TEDDY predicts proteins
  • that a readout of frozen TEDDY repairs the NK–T gap (v3 E3 rejected), or that a TEDDY layer localises it (v3 E5 not validated)
  • that gene-mean pooling loses the NK–T look-alike difference, or that TEDDY’s gene-mean state is sufficient for it: E5-M was inconclusive for the one observer tested
  • that ANM finds TEDDY’s minimal degrees of freedom: the retained state is declared, then tested
  • ANM’s field dynamics on TEDDY: every event enters at t = 0, so the field is a constant gain per class

Numbers now use the official TEDDY-G preprocessing (gene-median normalisation, top 2,048 rank tokens, mean over gene tokens); our earlier non-official preprocessing (no gene-median normalisation, 512 tokens) gave the same phase-1 Pearson.

不声称：ANM 让判定更准（v3 中与声明规则逐细胞相同）；TEDDY 预测蛋白；冻结 TEDDY 的读出能修复 NK–T 差距（v3 E3 拒绝），或某一层定位了它（v3 E5 未验证）；基因均值池化丢失了 NK–T 相似细胞的差异，或基因均值状态对其充分（E5-M 对所测的一个观察者判定为不确定）；ANM 找出了 TEDDY 的最小自由度（保留状态是先声明、后检验）；场动力学（所有事件在 t = 0）。数字现已改用 TEDDY-G 官方预处理（基因中位数归一化、前 2,048 个排序 token、基因 token 平均）；我们此前的非官方预处理（没有基因中位数归一化，512 个 token）给出相同的 phase-1 Pearson。
