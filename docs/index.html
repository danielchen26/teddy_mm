敲除引起的响应，TEDDY 留住了；接在它后面的蛋白头丢了近一半。TEDDY keeps the response to a knockout. The protein head built on it loses nearly half.

ANM 审计沿着模型自己的计算，一环一环地跟踪一个实测的响应，指出它从哪一步开始读不出来。在两套 Perturb-CITE-seq 数据上，这一步是 teddy_mm 蛋白头的第一段网络。修这一步，头多丢的部分在修复时没见过的敲除上去掉八到九成；修别处只去掉一成左右。修过的头还没有被证明不再丢。An ANM audit follows one measured response through each stage of a model's own computation and names the step where it stops being readable. On two Perturb-CITE-seq data sets that step is the first network of the teddy_mm protein head. Repairing that step removes 84% and 91% of what the head loses, on knockouts the repair never saw; repairing elsewhere removes 5% and 15%. No repaired head is yet shown to be free of the loss.

响应在哪一步丢Where the response is lost

系统是 TEDDY-G 70M 加上 teddy_mm 第一阶段从 RNA 预测 134 个蛋白的头。源是 IFN-γ 通路基因的 CRISPR 敲除，带非靶向对照。读出是 CITE-seq 实测的 PD-L1 蛋白，模型从没读过它。每一环问同一个问题：只凭这一环，实测的响应还有多少解释不了（u；容差 0.10）。The system is TEDDY-G 70M followed by the teddy_mm phase-1 head, which predicts 134 proteins from RNA. The source is CRISPR knockout of IFN-γ-pathway genes, with non-targeting controls. The readout is PD-L1 protein measured by CITE-seq, which the model never reads. Each stage is asked the same question: from this stage alone, how much of the measured response is left unexplained (u; margin 0.10).

黑色素瘤：嵌入得到支持（0.04，区间 −0.01 到 0.09），头的中间层被否定（0.20），输出 0.46。工具给出的定位是嵌入到头的中间层这一步。THP-1 上是同一步，但输入端本身没定清（5 个靶基因），所以算相对丢失。Melanoma: the embedding is supported (0.04, interval −0.01 to 0.09), the head latent is rejected (0.20) and the output is at 0.46. The tool locates the loss at the step from the embedding to the head latent. On THP-1 it is the same step, as a relative loss, because the input view itself is unresolved with five target genes.

闭环：定位，修，再审The loop: locate, repair, audit again

定位只是观察。要知道它对不对，就照它去改，再用同一把尺子量一遍。每一轮的设计和判定规则都在跑之前提交进仓库。三轮做完以后，又按区间把它们重读了一遍，下表两种读法都列出。A location is an observation. To learn whether it is right, change the model where it points and measure again with the same instrument. The design and the decision rule of every round were committed to the repository before the round was run. After the three rounds they were read again by intervals, and the table gives both readings.

能说什么，不能说什么What can and cannot be claimed

  • 能说（封存的检验）。在 Frangieh 2021 的 IFN-γ 通路敲除上，TEDDY-G 70M 的嵌入保留了解释 PD-L1 蛋白响应所需的信息（容差 0.10 以内）；五个蛋白读出上 TEDDY 没有一环被否定；teddy_mm 的头在嵌入和它的中间层之间丢掉了 PD-L1 的响应。Can claim (sealed tests). For the IFN-γ-pathway knockouts of Frangieh 2021, TEDDY-G 70M's embedding keeps the information that explains the PD-L1 protein response, within a 0.10 margin; no TEDDY stage is rejected for any of five protein readouts; the teddy_mm head loses the PD-L1 response between the embedding and its latent layer.
  • 探索性。把头拆开、把源拆开、类型化事件，以及闭环的各轮和对它们的重读：在留出的基因上，修头的 MLP 去掉头多丢的八到九成，修解码器去掉一成左右；按住骨髓输出后代价在 0.02 以内；THP-1 上修过的头确认没有多丢。Exploratory. The head split, the source separated, typed events, the rounds of the loop and their re-reading: on held-out genes, repairing the head's MLP removes 84% and 91% of what the head loses and repairing its decoder removes 5% and 15%; with the bone-marrow outputs held the cost stays within 0.02; on THP-1 the changed heads are shown to add no loss.
  • 不能说。头已经修好（没有一个修过的头被判“支持”，黑色素瘤上连没有多丢都没确认；修的是微调副本，一个种子）；一个头能同时管两种细胞；修复能带到第三种细胞；头为什么丢；TEDDY 对一般的扰动响应都够用；被否定的环“坏了”（否定只是在声明的读出模型下指出一步）。Cannot claim. That the head is repaired (no changed head is supported, and on melanoma not even the absence of an added loss is shown; the changed heads are fine-tuned copies, one seed); that one head serves both cell types; that the repair carries to a third cell type; why the head loses the response; that TEDDY is sufficient for perturbation responses in general; that a rejected stage is “broken” (a rejection names a step, under the declared readout classes).
