1 · Edit the question without retrain

Same frozen TEDDY evidence. Change the declared observer — not the backbone weights.

Story: criterion / observer edit

1 · Care about

Biologists often want a lineage-style call from the predicted surface panel: is this cell B, T, or myeloid? TEDDY foundation-model scientists train one RNA→protein map and freeze a readout head aimed at that scientific question.

2 · Expectation (without ANM)

A good answer looks like: maximize Pearson (~0.61 here), then apply one fixed decision rule forever. Same marker weights, same question, every week — no need to touch the map again.

3 · Problem framework

The question is not fixed. Collaborators change the criterion: O0 equal marker weight → O1 same labels but abstain more when the margin is thin → O2 prefer key-lineage markers. On site4/test, O0→O1 disagree rate 0.0000 (tighter abstain only); O0→O2 rewrites expected_action on 1,794 / 14,379 cells (rate 0.1248). A frozen Pearson head cannot rewrite that decision field — you retune with new labels, or keep answering last week’s question.

4 · How we solve (verified · HARD_PROOF)

TEDDY+ANM edits YAML only — abstain rises 317 → 834 → 3,373 (O0→O1→O2). TEDDY alone still answers with the O0 rule on 780 cells where O1 abstains and 1,752 where O2 abstains. Train+Jev (O0-trained logistic, zero-shot) keeps abstain = 185 on every observer; Q = 0.9552 / 0.9733 / 0.8277 — a confidence gate, not a declaration.

2 · Abstain when a modality is missing

Source ablation on the same base cells — reverse diagnosis, not a Pearson contest.

Story: missing-modality abstain / Pf tracking

1 · Care about

Real CITE batches are messy. Biologists care: when RNA or ADT is weak or offline, can I still trust a lineage / protein readout?

2 · Expectation (without ANM)

A good multimodal model always answers from whatever channels are present, and keeps Pearson high under joint RNA+ADT. Silence is failure; an answer is success.

3 · Problem framework

Same site4 cells, three masks: rna_only, adt_only, joint. Under weak adt_only, TEDDY-alone O0 abstain = 0 (silent over-answer) while Q ≈ 0.63. Always-answering ≠ trustworthy. Panel Pearson (rna_only≈0.915, adt_only≈0.446, joint≈0.870) is secondary; full-ADT phase-1 reference stays ~0.61.

4 · How we solve (verified · MISSING_MODALITY)

TEDDY+ANM Pf / abstain track the mask: rna_only abstain 233 (mean Pf 0.9815); adt_only abstain 1,664 (Pf 0.8675); joint abstain 0 (Pf 1.0). O2 under adt_only abstains on all 12,563. TEDDY alone O0 abstain = 0 on adt_only and joint. Train-retune under adt_only plateaus at Q≈0.68–0.70 even with 1,000 O2 labels — still no editable-field LOO (flip_rate≈0.71 under weak ADT).

3 · Attribute which marker flipped the call

Closed-form leave-one-out on the declared field — auditable per cell.

Story: declared-field attribution (LOO + flip)

1 · Care about

After a call is made, biologists ask: which surface marker actually flipped this cell’s decision? That is scientific attribution on the protein evidence — not a residual plot.

2 · Expectation (without ANM)

Once Pearson is good enough, use saliency / SHAP / “top expressed markers.” A good answer is a ranked feature list on opaque coefficients.

3 · Problem framework

A reviewer picks a T-lineage cell under O0: “If we drop CD5, does the call flip — and how far was the margin?” That needs leave-one-out on a declared decision field. Panel-mean heuristics and Train heads lack closed-form LOO / flip-distance on an editable observer. On full site4, top-1 flip concentration includes CD5 (3,882), CD2 (2,516), CD36 (2,394).

4 · How we solve (verified · HARD_PROOF)

TEDDY+ANM full-n LOO: n_attr=16,433, top-1 flip rate 0.277, flip-distance q50=0.75; bootstrap B=200 (mode CD5); permutation n_perm=100, one-sided p≈0.0099. TEDDY alone / Train+Jev: no declared-field LOO or flip distances in the proof — matching abstain numerically still does not buy attribution.

4 · Gate for conditional accuracy (scope refine)

Use continuous workability soft_P to spend a limited follow-up budget on trustworthy cells.

Story: soft_P / Pf coverage–Q gate

1 · Care about

Follow-up budget is limited. Biologists care: which cells are trustworthy enough to confirm or label next? Conditional correctness under partial coverage — not only a full-set average score.

2 · Expectation (without ANM)

Report one global accuracy (or Pearson) on the full test set. Every cell is equally worth answering; maximize average score at coverage = 1.

3 · Problem framework

A sorting experiment can confirm only ~40% of cells. Full-coverage Pearson hides barely-decided cells. Without a workability gate or attributed-hard scope, you cannot trade coverage for conditional correctness or prioritize which cells to label next.

4 · How we solve (verified · SCOPE_REFINE_PROOF)

Raise τ on soft_P = max(action_scores) so conditional Q climbs as coverage shrinks. Separately, ANM LOO marks flip-sensitive cells (harder scope): Q 0.8099 vs random same-n 0.9458. TEDDY+ANM soft_P gate: O0 baseline 0.9453 → peak 1.0000 (ΔQ +0.0547); O2 0.9149 → 0.9703 (ΔQ +0.0554); adt_only O0 0.6288 → 0.9494 (ΔQ +0.3206). At n=50 labels, hard-scoped training beats random on hard holdout (logreg ΔQ ≈ +0.034). TEDDY alone “always answer” is the cov=1 baseline; Train can fit a head on hard labels but lacks the declared soft_P field that produced the scope.

5 · Zero-label criterion transfer vs label-cost calibration

Train can numerically match ANM’s O2 operating point — only by burning O2 labels, and still without an editable field.

Story: zero-label observer transfer

1 · Care about

Mid-project the scoring rubric changes (O0→O2). Biologists and FM teams care: can we adopt the new criterion without a new labeling campaign?

2 · Expectation (without ANM)

Adapt by collecting more labels and fitting a small head / calibrator. Cost = labeled cells; success = matching accuracy / abstain numbers.

3 · Problem framework

Even after spending ≈50–500 O2 labels to match abstain≈0.20 and strict≈0.82, Train still has no editable-field attribution and must re-spend labels on the next observer edit. Numerical match ≠ criterion ownership.

4 · How we solve (verified · HARD_PROOF)

ANM already sits at abstain≈0.201, strict≈0.821 with zero new endpoint labels. To match that operating point: grid ≈50 labels; MLP ≈200; logistic / Jev stand-in ≈500. ANM: 0 endpoint labels + editable field. O0-trained Train abstain stays 185 across O0/O1/O2 until retuned.
