Prior predictors freeze one question.
ANM edits the observer — without retraining TEDDY.

A typical supervised head locks the decision criterion at train time. Change the question → retrain + new labels. Weak modality → it still answers. No editable field, no abstain that follows a declaration, no auditable attribution. ANM sits on typed evidence from a frozen TEDDY and turns the criterion into a declared field you can rewrite in YAML.

What prior predictors could not do

Same frozen TEDDY δu. The gaps below are structural — not “train harder.”

  • Cannot Change the question without retraining. O0→O1 tightens abstain; O0→O2 rewrites expected_action on 12.48% of cells. A fixed head needs new labels + retune for each observer.
  • Cannot Abstain when evidence is weak. Under adt_only, TEDDY-alone O0 abstain = 0 (silent over-answer). ANM O0 abstain = 1,664; O2 abstains on all 12,563.
  • Cannot Attribute decision flips on a declared field. Panel-mean heuristics have no closed-form LOO / flip distance. ANM reports full-n LOO (n_attr=16,433), flip rate 0.277, permutation p≈0.0099.
  • Cannot Gate for conditional accuracy without a new model. Soft_P = max(action_scores) raises conditional Q as coverage tightens (O0 peak ΔQ +0.0547; adt_only ΔQ_peak +0.3206) — SCOPE_REFINE_PROOF.
  • Cannot Transfer criteria at zero label cost. To numerically match ANM’s O2 operating point (abstain≈0.20, strict≈0.82), Train needs ≈50 / 200 / 500 O2 labels (grid / MLP / logistic) — and still has no editable-field attribution.

Where ANM sits

ANM consumes typed evidence built from TEDDY protein-panel predictions, verified against holdout GT (adt_true = verifier only). Observers O0 / O1 / O2 are YAML criteria (bridge_anm/readouts/cite_lineage_O{0,1,2}.yaml). Editing the observer = edit the declaration — zero endpoint-label fit, no best.pt retrain.

Phase-1 TEDDY+MLP full-ADT Pearson on the export slice: 0.610 (~0.61). Missing-modality demo uses hybrid export (phase-1 RNA + phase-2 ADT) because phase-2 RNA Pearson ≈0.25–0.28 ≪ 0.61. Panel Pearson by mask: rna_only≈0.915, adt_only≈0.446, joint≈0.870 — secondary; not the win condition.

4 · Conditional accuracy via Pf / soft_P gate (SCOPE)

Continuous workability soft_P = max(action_scores) raises conditional Q as coverage tightens. Flip-sensitive cells (ANM LOO flipped) are a harder scope: Q 0.8099 vs random same-n 0.9458 (ΔQ −0.1359). At n=50 labels, training on ANM flip-hard cells beats random labels on hard holdout (logreg ΔQ ≈ +0.034).

Superiority axes — and what we refuse to claim

O0→O2 expected_action rewrite 12.48% via YAML only. Train/TEDDY-alone need labels + retune.

Abstain

Attribution

Zero-label transfer
