# The external PBMC study after E6: fitted on from 16 November 2026

A note, not an addendum of the v3 registration. It changes no registered endpoint, cell set or result. `HASHES.txt`
does not list it.

**What E6 fixed.** Hao et al. 2021 (GEO GSE164378, the 3′ CITE-seq PBMC set) was registered as the external
confirmation data of experiment E6. Its pack marks every cell `split = "test"`, and
`scripts/prepare_external_cite.py` says of these cells: "evaluation-only, nothing may be fit on them". E6 is final;
its results are in `docs/reports/V3_REGISTERED_RESULTS.md`.

**What changes.** From 16 November 2026 cells of this study are fitted on, in
[`anm_diag/runs/round1_coverage_20261116`](../../anm_diag/runs/round1_coverage_20261116/DESIGN.md): PBMC cells of
some of its donors are added to the training of new heads, and its other donors are used to develop that change. The
owner of the repository approved this on that day.

**What does not change.**

- E6 and every number reported for it. They were computed with the head of that date, which was never fitted on
  these cells.
- The pack. `split` stays `"test"` for every cell, so the registered scripts read it as before. The round's own
  trainer chooses its cells by donor and does not read `split` for this study.
- The head in use (`outputs/cite_phase1_official`). The round fits new heads beside it.

**What follows.**

- For any head fitted in that round or after it on cells of this study, the study confirms nothing. It is fit and
  development data.
- External confirmation of such a head needs a study that has not been used. The round names one, opens it once, and
  says how it was chosen.
- Any later text that calls this study "external" says for which head.
